This is the order builder, styled to live inside Cerbo's chart. I'm acting as Doctor Welby. I pick Zeus King, and add Omega-3 and vitamin D.

Prices pre-fill from the practice default. I can override a line, or type a target margin and let the price derive. The billing panel shows the split live: order total, cost of goods, the platform fee, and the provider's margin after the fee.

When I send it, the order locks, and the patient gets what Cerbo would actually send: a secure portal message, and an email nudge.
