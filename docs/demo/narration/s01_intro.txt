This is a take-home for Cerbo: an in-house supplement store, built as a vertical slice in Cerbo's own stack. Vue, TypeScript, Fastify, and Postgres.

The brief is simple to state and easy to get wrong. A provider assembles a supplement order, the patient pays, and the system records exactly where every cent goes: cost of goods, the provider's margin, and Cerbo's seventy-five basis point fee.
