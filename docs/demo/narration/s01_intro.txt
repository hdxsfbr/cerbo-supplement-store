This is a take-home for Cerbo: an in-house supplement store, built as a vertical slice in Cerbo's own stack. Vue, TypeScript, Fastify, and Postgres.

The brief: a provider orders supplements for a patient, the patient pays, and the system records exactly where every cent goes. Cost of goods, the provider's margin, and Cerbo's seventy-five basis point fee.
