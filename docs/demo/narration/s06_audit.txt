This is the part that's graded. The ledger holds exactly three entries for the paid order, each with a memo showing its own computation.

The fee is seventy-five basis points of the total. Here that lands on a half cent, and it rounds half-up. The margin is the residual, so the three always sum to what the patient paid.

And it isn't just the application saying so. A deferred constraint trigger in Postgres re-checks the whole split at commit. The ledger refuses updates and deletes. The test suite proves it by trying to break the split with raw SQL, and watching the database refuse.
